## Scene Breakdown & Beat Choreography

### Scene 1: The Runaway Token Cascade (0.0s – 3.2s)
- **Visuals**: A terminal prompt executes an autonomous multi-agent recursive loop. A real-time token counter spins out of control from `0` to `428,000` tokens. A digital billing counter rapidly increments from `$0.10` to `$14.85` in red numbers.
- **Anchor Motion**: In the center, faint ghost outlines of the Chrono-Vault Aegis begin to rotate, sensing high token velocity.
- **Typography**: Kinetic staggered text: `"AN INFINITE LOOP IS RUNNING IN YOUR STAGING CLUSTER."`
- **SFX**: Accelerating digital clock ticks, low humming oscillator rising in pitch.

### Scene 2: The Circuit Breaker Tripped (3.2s – 6.8s)
- **Visuals**: At exactly `$5.0000`, heavy mechanical hydraulic brake calipers clamp down on the token stream. The Chrono-Vault Aegis snaps together with interlocking gold facets. The core flashes from emerald `#10B981` to searing crimson `#EF4444`.
- **Telemetry Overlay**: `HTTP 429 TOO MANY REQUESTS: CIRCUIT BREAKER TRIPPED. RUNAWAY HALTED.`
- **Typography**: Bold typography snaps in: `"ZERO OVERAGES. ZERO CLOUD BILL SURPRISES."`
- **SFX**: Heavy industrial relay latch "CLANK-SNAP!", followed by an immediate dead-silence bass drop.

### Scene 3: The Avant-Garde Telemetry Radar (6.8s – 11.4s)
- **Visuals**: Camera pulls back into an isometric perspective of the dark-glassmorphic Web Deck. Live SVG arcs track model spending distribution (GPT-4o, Claude 3.5 Sonnet, DeepSeek R1). Real-time SSE transactions glide across a Bento grid. A slider sets an emergency ceiling at `$10.00`.
- **Anchor Motion**: The Aegis floats in the header, pulsating with a rhythmic emerald heartbeat as healthy mocked and proxied requests pass seamlessly.
- **Typography**: `"INLINE COST CIRCUIT BREAKER • 100% OFFLINE ZERO-DEPENDENCY RUNTIME"`.
- **SFX**: High-tech tactile click transients, soothing ambient synth pad.

### Scene 4: Sovereign Call to Action & Founding Access (11.4s – 15.0s)
- **Visuals**: Glassmorphic card emerges with dual Stripe and Solana payment badges. Strikethrough pricing `$79` drops to `$19 Lifetime Founding License`. A single-line curl command displays: `agent-budget run -- npx my-agent`.
- **Centerpiece**: The Chrono-Vault Aegis glows in polished sovereign gold `#D4AF37` with a rotating conic border beam.
- **Typography**: `"PROTECT YOUR TREASURY. RUN AUTONOMOUSLY."`
- **Attribution**: `"Engineered by Neal Frazier Tech (@1nc0gn30) • NullAI Platform"`
- **SFX**: Warm sub-bass resolution chime, stereo tape-stop exit.
